# Horizon Money

[![CI](https://github.com/CastilhoBorges/horizon-money/actions/workflows/ci.yml/badge.svg)](https://github.com/CastilhoBorges/horizon-money/actions/workflows/ci.yml)
[![OpenSSF Scorecard](https://api.scorecard.dev/projects/github.com/CastilhoBorges/horizon-money/badge)](https://scorecard.dev/viewer/?uri=github.com/CastilhoBorges/horizon-money)
[![License: MIT](https://img.shields.io/badge/license-MIT-blue.svg)](LICENSE)

Open-source, self-hosted personal-finance forecasting that you connect to your AI agent through MCP.

Log what comes in and what goes out, and Horizon Money projects your balance day by day for the months
ahead. Plug it into Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor or any MCP client and just talk to it:
"I spent 30 on lunch", "how much will I have on the 15th?", "close my month".

> **Status:** early development (Phase 0 — foundation). The design lives in [`docs/specs/`](docs/specs/)
> and the plan in the [milestones](https://github.com/CastilhoBorges/horizon-money/milestones).
